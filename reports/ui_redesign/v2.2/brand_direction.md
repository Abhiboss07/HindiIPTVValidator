# T2L V2.2 — Brand Direction

## 1. Brand Philosophy

T2L is a cinematic media space engineered for high-fidelity personal entertainment. The brand represents precision transmission, uncompromised fidelity, and curated cinematic discovery.

The identity must stand shoulder-to-shoulder with the most respected names in contemporary technology and cinema design:
* **Restraint over excess**: No loud decorative flourishes or superficial gradients that distract from content.
* **Architecture over decoration**: Every line, angle, and radius must have mathematical intention.
* **Media-first**: The identity recedes into the background when content plays, yet remains unmistakable in small interfaces, watermarks, app icons, and notifications.

---

## 2. Brand Dimensions

| Attribute | What T2L Is | What T2L Is NOT |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Form** | Geometric, architectural, monolithic, streamlined | Ornate, organic, whimsical, skeuomorphic |
| **Motion** | Fluid, purposeful, linear-eased, composited | Bouncy, spinning, explosive, flash-in-the-pan |
| **Personality** | Confident, cinematic, calm, editorial | Gaming-focused, hyperactive, noisy, generic |
| **Metaphor** | Projection gate, light transmission, unified monogram | Camera lens, play button, film strip, Wi-Fi antenna |

## 4. Color Modes & Surface Adaptability

The T2L mark is designed to exist in five operational surface modes:

1. **OLED Mode (`#000000`)**: Pure, high-contrast monochrome white (`#FFFFFF`) with zero halos, maximizing power efficiency on mobile OLED displays.
2. **Aurora Mode**: A subtle 135° linear gradient running from deep violet (`#8B5CF6`) to electric cyan (`#22D3EE`), reflecting the core streaming aura.
3. **Monochrome Inverted (`#FFFFFF`)**: Deep charcoal mark (`#0B0E14`) on pure white surfaces (press, print, documentation).
4. **Dark Neutral Mode**: Subdued zinc tone (`#71717A`) for inactive, watermarked, or background player states.
5. **App Icon Context**: Encapsulated within a squircle tile (`rx=11` at 48px, `rx=22%` proportional) with a subtle inner border (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`) and radial depth glow.
