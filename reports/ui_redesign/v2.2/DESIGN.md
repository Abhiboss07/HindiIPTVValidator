# T2L V2.2 — Master Design Document

## 1. Vision & Core Objectives

T2L V2.2 is an isolated visual redesign iteration focusing strictly on:
1. **The Brand Identity**: Introducing "The Nexus Ribbon" mark and precision `T2L` wordmark.
2. **The Cinema Experience**: Elevating Cinema into an editorial, curated cinematic discovery pavilion with 5 distinct card types, atmospheric lighting, and responsive shelf architecture.

All other components (Home, Live TV, Radio, Local Vault, Footer, Navigation Dock, and System Modals) remain frozen from the approved V2.1 baseline.

---

## 2. Brand Identity: "The Nexus Ribbon"

* **Architecture**: A singular mathematical monogram intertwining the letters `T`, `2`, and `L` across a 48×48 coordinate grid.
* **Symbolism**:
  - Horizontal top beam: Widescreen theatrical horizon.
  - Kinetic diagonal: Light transmission and projection beam.
  - Anchored base & vertical uplift: Grounded high-fidelity reproduction.
* **Miniaturization**: 100% legible at 16×16px; zero muddiness or fine detail collapse.
* **Freedom from Clichés**: No play button triangles, no camera lenses, no film reels, no TV antennae.

---

## 3. Cinema Architecture: The Curated Pavilion

* **Atmospheric Premiere Showcase**: Widescreen featured hero with ambient theatrical illumination and real metadata (*Kalki 2898 AD*).
* **Curated Discovery Modes**: Replacing generic segmented chips with architectural modes: `[PREMIERES | THEATRICAL | SAGAS]`.
* **Theatrical Horizons Rail**: Continuous horizontal scroll featuring high-impact landscape cards.
* **Curated Editorial Shelves**:
  - *Now Showing in Theatres*
  - *Cinematic Epics & Sagas*
  - *Modern Masterpieces (2024–2026)*
  - *The Vault Classics & Public Domain*
* **Five Card Types**:
  - Type A: Featured Premiere Widescreen Card
  - Type B: Standard Theatrical 2:3 Poster Card
  - Type C: Panoramic Series / Saga Card
  - Type D: Continue Watching 16:9 Card with real progress fill
  - Type E: Curated Editorial Collection Banner
* **Smooth Detail Transition**: Seamless card-to-backdrop expand transition into full-bleed movie details.

---

## 4. Responsive Hierarchy

* **Mobile (320px – 430px)**: 2-column theatrical grid, full-bleed hero, touch-friendly shelf scroll with 2.2 card peeks.
* **Tablet (768px – 834px)**: 3–4 columns with spacious horizontal margins and balanced typography.
* **Desktop (1024px – 1440px)**: 5–6 columns, cinematic widescreen presentation, and ambient backdrop reflections.
